# Welcome to NSDAHR

**NSDAHR (NSDA Helpful Resources)** is an educational platform designed specifically for NSDA students and self-learners mastering Web Development.

---

## 🎯 Platform Objectives

- **Structured Learning:** Well-organized Q&A guides covering HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
- **Exam & Interview Prep:** Focused on essential concepts asked in technical evaluations and assessments.
- **Clean UI & Instant Access:** Fast, responsive, and easy to navigate across all devices.

---

## 📚 What You Will Learn

1. **HTML5 & Web Semantics**
   - Semantic tags, document layout, accessibility, and form validation.

2. **CSS3 & Responsive Layouts**
   - Box Model, Flexbox vs Grid, media queries, and responsive design patterns.

3. **JavaScript & Modern ES6+**
   - DOM manipulation, event delegation, closures, scope, Promises, and Async/Await.
